Keep the narration clearly identified as synthetic if retained; no audio-led claim or pacing beat counts as proof.

## Dramatic question and truthful claim

**Question:** After someone corrects a private reflection, can they explore a relevant peer story and choose exactly what happens next?

The film proves visible local preview behavior only: a person changes editable wording, opens prepared fictional material, changes a question in a local audience preview, and selects a private choice. It does not prove that an AI produced the reflection, that a peer is present or responded, that a provider is available, or that anything persists beyond this preview session. Keep “prepared fictional example,” “no model connected,” and “nothing sent” adjacent to the relevant moment. Show Muse Spark once as future work: it could organize themes into a draft for the person to review, edit, or discard; it is not connected here.

## Picture and edit rules

- Keep v22’s uninterrupted correction capture intact as the centerpiece. Do not fake a continuous take if capture continuity breaks; identify a cut plainly if one is unavoidable.
- Record the post-correction path from the functioning local UI. Actions must cause the next visible state. Cursor movement, typing, selecting, scrolling to evidence, and returning are the motion; avoid zoom animations or decorative transitions that imply product behavior.
- Make the peer story an openable, inspectable example. Put its match and difference in the same readable view, then show the person choosing what to do with that information. Do not call the match a recommendation, score, or evidence about real people.
- Keep the exact question, group, alias, excluded private context, consent state, and disabled action legible together. Do not select consent, submit, post, or show a simulated response.
- Keep labels concise and adjacent: one provenance label when entering prepared material, one unconnected label for the local reflection, and one no-send label at the disclosure choice. Do not stack repeated caveats over the opening.
- No actor, portrait, fake inference, fabricated live activity, typing indicator, provider outcome, peer reply, sent message, or fictional life result. The family decision remains unresolved.
- The full story must work muted. Captions state the practical meaning of each action and the outcome visible on screen. Keep the synthetic soundtrack secondary; record that audio remains inaccessible to the cold reviewer unless a separate reviewer can actually audition it.

## Acceptance gates for the v23 export

1. **Opening comprehension:** At 0:05, a muted viewer can identify the family-pressure problem, private reflection promise, and fictional/no-model status. Remove stacked copy; retain readable labels at 960×540.
2. **Continuous correction evidence:** The exported shot shows the entered words, **Not quite**, the edited wording, and the changed reflection in one uninterrupted sequence. The change is readable without narration. No frozen result exceeds five seconds.
3. **Peer relevance and consequence:** The prepared story’s provenance, match, and difference are visible together. The next action is an actual supported UI state reached by a visible selection. No implication of live social activity or a guaranteed response.
4. **Disclosure consequence:** Revising the question visibly revises the preview. Audience, alias, private-context exclusion, unchecked consent, disabled action, and no-send status are readable at 960×540. No send action occurs.
5. **Support boundary:** Provider availability is stated in the actual help route in one concise readable line, alongside an action returning to the user’s choices. No provider is presented as verified or bookable.
6. **Ending:** The final visible product state is the selected private choice, with session-only scope and nothing shared. The cut ends promptly after it reads; no unanswered thread or static UI lingers.
7. **Pacing and access:** No static hold exceeds five seconds. Captions are concise, timed to actions, no more than two lines, and clear of controls and critical copy. Every substantive claim survives muted playback.
8. **Duration and integrity:** Target 176 seconds only if each beat advances an action or reveals its consequence; otherwise shorten to the smallest truthful cut. If kept at 176–179 seconds, confirm full decode, synchronized streams, caption timing, and the exact selected closing state in the export.
