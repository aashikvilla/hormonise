# Revised video-only evaluation protocol

These prompts define video-only cold-judge protocol v2.1. The judge evaluates technical depth and all other rubric criteria solely from what the candidate video shows or audibly states when audio is accessible. The fixed weighted rubric total remains separate from a new 0–100 film-persuasion score, calculated from four equally weighted, timestamp-evidenced dimensions. The 85-point target applies to film persuasion and must never inflate either score.

Historical versions v01–v04 were scored under the original protocol. Their reports and scores remain unchanged and must not be retroactively reinterpreted. Apply v2.1 to a new candidate review only, passing the judge the candidate video and updated rubric alone.
