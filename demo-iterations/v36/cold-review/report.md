Muse Spark is planned to organize a correctable reflection, with optional voice transcription (0:00–0:30). The proposed value is helping someone find language while retaining the right to correct it. The film shows a designed reflection and route toward peers, not model reasoning, calls, transcription, or reliability. 1. **0:00–0:15 — Sharpen the promise.** Use “Find words for a difficult family conversation—privately, on your terms,” then show correction with a fictional-preview disclosure. Verify a first-time viewer can name the problem and promise after the opening.
2. **0:14–0:30 — Show one interaction.** In one continuous shot, select “Not quite,” edit, and show the reflection update; label it prepared prototype behavior. Verify input and state change are visible without implying AI execution.
3. **0:36–0:56 — Reduce the social tour.** Show one peer response and host boundary as prepared examples. Verify both roles and the label fit one short screen.
4. **About 1:00–1:28 — Compress sharing and support.** Pair the audience preview with unchecked consent and make provider limitations a short card. Verify group, alias, text, exclusions, and no-send state are legible.
5. **About 1:30–2:06 — End on choice.** Select “Maybe later,” retain the private takeaway, and close on “Nothing is sent.” Verify the draft stays unsent and reflection private.

## Judge reactions and release recommendation

**Skeptical objection:** This is a thoughtful interface for a real family-pressure problem, but the film shows prepared copy and fictional people. The proposed Meta model is not connected, so its central technical contribution is untested. The peer network has no live members, and the benefit is a designed scenario rather than an observed outcome. Why should this beat private journaling with a hypothetical model layer?

**Best-case reaction:** The team understands that a sensitive decision should stay with the person, not an AI or crowd. The person can correct a possible interpretation, explore peer experiences at their pace, inspect an exact audience and message, or stop without sending. The disclosures are unusually honest, and the unresolved examples avoid promising that community support fixes the problem. A concise prototype interaction and truthful Meta-model demonstration could make this distinctive.

**Release recommendation: Maybe; do not present this as a live AI or community demo.** The blockers to judging demonstrated execution are the absent evidence for the planned model, entirely prepared social examples, and a film that spends too long on dense pages to make its human decision memorable. Keep the candid labels; show a verifiable correction in one continuous sequence; and either demonstrate an actual model call truthfully or describe that role only as proposed.
