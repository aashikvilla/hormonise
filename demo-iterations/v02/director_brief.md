# Creative direction: v02 → v03

**Recommendation: make a narrowly scoped v03 as a truthful preview film.** v02 is a small regression in the cold-judge totals from v01 (58/100 vs 59/100) and a larger regression in film persuasion (66 vs 73). It gained clarity and polish: demo clarity rose from 3/6 to 5/6, and UI polish from 2/3 to 3/3. It lost a point each in impact, named Meta model roles (3/7 to 0/7), and architecture/failure handling (3/5 to 2/5). The better legibility and cleaner disclosures are real gains, but they did not make the story more persuasive overall.

Both cold judges reviewed sampled pictures and captions without audio. Their conclusions cannot assess voice delivery or narration. The v02 verifier independently confirms that the 0:06–0:13 narration says advice came before Arjun could explain, while the picture shows only his prepared Day 1 exchange and no advice or specific need. This is a genuine opening mismatch and the sole v02 release blocker. v01's release blocker was different: its “restricted workflow” narration contradicted the publicly reachable illustrative moderator screen. v02 corrected that line and passes the moderator disclosure check.

The Meta-name finding is a visibility/readability gap, not a fully absent slate: v02’s closing architecture card visibly names “Future Muse Spark” and “Muse Voice Transcribe,” and the verifier confirms the planned / not-connected disclosure. The cold judge still gave 0/7 for named model roles, likely because names and roles are confined to a brief closing card and its narration could not be heard. Make the names and planned roles unmistakable in the picture. This must remain a capability plan, not a claim that either integration is running.
