## Credibility, model attribution, accessibility, and craft

All dialogue is prepared text, not organic participant speech. Fictional labels are candid, though the Day 1/4/9/16 arc implies growth without interaction or testimony. No Meta integration is claimed: AI triage is explicitly disconnected, and no model, role, or model-specific output appears. Transcript correction and community discovery remain proposals.

The restrained palette, headings, action labels, and warnings are coherent. Body copy and tiny context markers may be hard to read at normal playback size. Burned-in captions are high contrast, but synchronization, accuracy against speech, narration, and sound cannot be judged. Pause behavior is not evidenced. “You can read without replying” is the emotional high point; the simulated moderation cut is the low point.

## Five highest-impact fix tickets

1. **0:00–0:15 — State the product promise sooner.** Add one sentence naming the family-pressure audience, private reflection, and optional peer perspectives. **Expected:** clear differentiation in five seconds. **Verify:** ask first-time viewers to describe the product after the opening without narration.
2. **0:15–0:35 — Prove one model interaction.** Show a live, repeatable Meta model interaction, name its role, and show a user correction changing the generated result. **Expected:** credible necessity and attribution. **Verify:** rerun the input and a correction; compare output and model trace.
3. **0:40–1:05 — Explain matching.** Translate context markers into plain-language reasons and let users change them. **Expected:** inspectable recommendations. **Verify:** change a preference and show explained results update.
4. **1:10–1:35 — Demonstrate sharing controls.** Show authentication, alias, audience preview, cancellation, persistence, and data movement. **Expected:** enforceable privacy. **Verify:** cancel and confirm nothing reaches the recipient or storage.
5. **1:40–2:20 — Make review operational.** Show a functioning report queue, reviewer permissions, escalation, and urgent-help path. **Expected:** clear harm response and boundaries with clinical care. **Verify:** submit and process a report; test unauthorized access and escalation.

## Skeptical objection

This is an elegant set of static scenarios with no connected AI, live peers, real moderation, or enforced staff permissions. Its central promise depends on exactly those missing services, and the film provides no evidence that a Meta model is needed or used. A polished prototype can conceal the hardest product questions: privacy, recommendation quality, harmful advice, and operational response. On this evidence, shortlist credit would reward design intent as implementation.

## Best-case reaction and release recommendation

The best case is a humane design for people who need language and perspective before acting. Silence, editing, leaving, audience preview, and matching explanations are strong foundations. Continue development and resubmit after an end-to-end flow shows attributed Meta model use, enforceable privacy/moderation, and live interaction. Current blockers are disconnected AI/voice, prepared conversation, absent community, and illustrative review.
